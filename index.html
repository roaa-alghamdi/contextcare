One solution, two connected interfaces

STC already gives every complaint a reference number. ContextCare makes that number carry the full context, adds AI decision support for staff, and shows customers the same live status.

Reference-number lookup

One number retrieves the complaint, the customer details and the full record – the customer never repeats information.

AI complaint summary

An automatic summary appears on the first screen, so any employee understands the issue in seconds.

Smart routing & risk

AI predicts escalation risk, recommends the right department and suggests the next best action.

Live tracker in mystc

How the customer tracker works

The tracker reads the same data as the employee dashboard, so the customer always sees the same status as the employee. Try it:

  • On track (green): shows the stage and the time remaining, e.g. “Resolution in 2 days”.
  • Overdue (red): an SLA alert apologizes for the delay and lets the customer escalate or contact technical support with the ticket number visible.
  • Resolved (blue): the customer rates the resolution from 1 to 5 stars.

Escalate a complaint or submit a rating here, then sign out and sign in as an employee: the action appears instantly in the complaint record, and the rating feeds the satisfaction indicator.

Survey evidence behind this feature

  • 91 of 187 complainants ranked the current status as the most important information when following up.
  • 45.7% of customers with continuity issues found their complaint status unclear.
